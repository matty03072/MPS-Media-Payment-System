PayPal subscriptions

Webhook URL: {{ public_url }}/paypal/webhook

Required events: PAYMENT.SALE.COMPLETED, PAYMENT.SALE.REFUNDED, PAYMENT.SALE.REVERSED, BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.PAYMENT.FAILED, BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.ACTIVATED, BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.CANCELLED, BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.SUSPENDED and BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.EXPIRED.

Enabling validates the configured webhook against PayPal. Publish monthly plans to PayPal from the overview. The environment and merchant application are fixed once plans or checkout records exist; use a separate installation for sandbox testing.

Approval alone never enables access. Successful live payment enables the paid period. Failed renewal cancels further collection; access ends at the existing paid-through date. Cancellation stops renewal without removing already-paid access.
