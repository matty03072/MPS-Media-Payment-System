Your monthly subscription

MPS costs £5 per month for up to three registered applications. It renews monthly until cancelled. This subscription is separate from payments you collect from your own members.

Cancel without another renewal

Sign in to your licence account, choose Cancel subscription, confirm your password and submit the cancellation. No reason is required. We schedule cancellation at the end of your current billing period. Your account shows the request status and access-end date. If processing is delayed, we retry and show that status; contact support if you need help.

14-day refund promise

If you request cancellation within 14 days after your latest successful subscription payment, we refund that payment in full to the original payment method. This applies to the initial payment and subsequent monthly payments. A payment cannot be refunded twice. Your account shows whether a refund is pending, succeeded or needs attention; your bank may take additional time to display it.

After 14 days, our voluntary policy does not provide a refund for that payment, but renewal still stops at the billing period end. This does not limit any statutory right to a refund, remedy or cancellation.

When the licence ends

Your licence stays active until the billing period end, including when a payment is refunded under this promise. At that time the unlock ends and MPS returns to its free ten-member allowance. Account data is retained. Removing an application registration is different: it disables that application and frees a registration slot but does not cancel billing.

Alternative cancellation request

You can send the following through support or to the seller contact shown above: “I give notice that I cancel my MPS subscription. Licence-account email: ____. Order reference: ____. Request date: ____.” We use the time your cancellation request is received to assess the 14-day window. Do not include your password or unlock code.

Scope and responsibility

This notice covers the MPS licence purchase, account, activation and support service. If you operate your own MPS installation, you are responsible for the personal data you collect from your own members. Their media passwords and library activity are not required by this licensing service.

Information we use

  • Account email, password hash, verification status, encrypted MFA setup information and hashed recovery codes.
  • Order and subscription references, payment status, amount, currency, payment dates, cancellation and refund records. Card details are entered with Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Application UUIDs, registration/check-in IP addresses, last-seen times and registration/removal history.
  • Reply email and issue details you submit to support, together with delivery status.
  • Security, service and anti-abuse records needed to operate and protect the service.

Why we use it

We use account, purchase, licence and support information to perform our contract with you. We use proportionate security and fraud-prevention information for our legitimate interests in protecting accounts and enforcing application allowances. We retain records where necessary to meet legal accounting or other obligations. We do not use support submissions for marketing.

Who receives information

Stripe processes payments and refunds. Cloudflare provides network protection and traffic delivery. Our email-delivery provider processes account/support messages. These providers receive only the information needed for their role. Authorised service administrators can access information needed for support and security. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to handle a legal claim. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and security

Account and registration records are kept while needed to provide the licence service. Payment, cancellation and refund records may be retained after closure where needed for accounting, disputes or legal obligations. Support and security records are retained only for as long as needed to resolve requests, investigate abuse or defend claims. Retention depends on those purposes and applicable obligations; contact us to request review or deletion. Backups may retain information until their normal replacement cycle.

We use access controls, password hashing, MFA, encrypted stored secrets and HTTPS. No service can promise absolute security. Do not submit passwords, API keys, payment card details or unlock codes in support messages.

Cookies and automated decisions

Essential session cookies keep you signed in and protect forms. The licence service uses automated checks of subscription status and registered applications to grant or limit access. Contact support if you believe a decision is wrong. No advertising cookies are required by these MPS licence pages; Stripe operates its own payment pages under its privacy information.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where a lawful obligation or exception applies. Contact us using the details above; licence customers can use the signed-in support form. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

International processing and changes

Our service providers operate internationally, including outside the UK. Their published data-protection terms describe applicable transfer safeguards and processing arrangements: Stripe, Cloudflare and SMTP2GO. You can request more information or a copy of relevant safeguards using the seller contact details above. We will update this notice when our processing changes and bring material changes to your attention.
